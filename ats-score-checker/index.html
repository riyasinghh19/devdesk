    What the score measures

    Applicant tracking systems (ATS) are the software many companies use to sort applications. Each one works a little differently, so no checker can promise a result. This one checks the things recruiters and ATS software consistently reward:

    CheckPointsWhat we look for
    Keyword match40Skills, tools and phrases from the job description that appear in your resume.
    Sections15Contact details, summary, experience, education and skills under clear headings.
    Measurable impact15Bullets with numbers: time saved, money, volume, percentages.
    Action verbs10Bullets that start with verbs like Built, Led, Reduced or Automated.
    Length10Roughly 400 to 1,000 words, about one to two pages.
    Clarity10No vague phrases like "responsible for", no "I/my", no overlong bullets.

    How to raise your score

    • Mirror the job's wording. If the job says "Azure Data Factory", write that, not "ADF" alone.
    • Put numbers in your bullets. "Cut report run time from 40 to 6 minutes" beats "Improved performance".
    • Use standard headings. "Experience" and "Education" are read correctly. Creative headings often aren't.
    • Keep the layout simple. Tables, text boxes and icons can confuse ATS parsers. Our Resume Builder exports a clean, single-column PDF.

    Frequently asked questions

    What do I get for ₹169?

    Your score out of 100 with a breakdown, the missing and matched keywords, and a list of fixes. The payment covers up to 5 checks in the same browser tab for 48 hours, so you can edit and re-check.

    Does it use AI?

    Partly. The score itself comes from fixed, consistent rules, so the same resume always gets the same score. On top of that, an AI model (Llama, running on Cloudflare) spots skills you list under different wording, sums up how well you fit the job, and rewrites a few weak bullets. Anything the AI claims about your resume is checked against your actual text before we show it.

    Is my resume stored?

    No. Your resume and the job description are sent to our server only to calculate the report and are not saved. We keep only the payment reference, so we can count your checks.

    Which files can I upload?

    PDF, Word (.docx) and plain text. The file is read in your browser. If a scanned PDF shows no text, paste your resume text instead.

    Can I get a refund?

    If you paid and didn't get your report because of a problem on our side, email us within 7 days and we'll refund you in full. See the refund policy.

    Does a high score guarantee interviews?

    No. It means your resume is clear, complete and closely matched to the job, which gives it the best chance of reaching a person.
