Daylight saving: the twice-a-year surprise

India doesn't change its clocks, but the UK, Europe and the US do, on different dates. For a few weeks in March and in October–November, the gap to the US shrinks by an hour before Europe catches up. If a recurring meeting suddenly feels an hour off, this is why. Pick the meeting date above and the planner uses the right offset for that day.

  • US: clocks go forward on the second Sunday of March and back on the first Sunday of November.
  • UK and EU: forward on the last Sunday of March, back on the last Sunday of October.

Frequently asked questions

Why is IST "+5:30" and not a whole hour?

India uses one time zone based on 82.5° E longitude, which is 5 hours 30 minutes ahead of UTC. A few other places, like Nepal (+5:45), also use non-whole offsets.

Does it work for any city?

The list covers the most common business cities. "Add my time zone" adds whatever zone your device is set to.
