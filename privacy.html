Privacy policy

This policy explains what DevDesk (the "site") collects and how it is used.

Data you enter into tools

Free tools run entirely in your web browser. SQL, text, files and numbers you enter are processed on your device and are not sent to or stored on our servers.

Pro tools and payments

The Resume Builder and ATS Score Checker send the resume details or text and the job description you provide to our server, only to create your PDF or report. This content is not stored. Payments are handled by Razorpay, which receives your payment details directly; we never see your card or UPI details. We keep the payment reference for up to 7 days to count how many times it has been used.

Preferences

The site stores small preferences, such as light or dark mode and your last chosen tool settings, in your browser's local storage. You can clear them at any time from your browser settings.

Advertising

The site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, and learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Analytics

The site may use privacy-friendly analytics to count visits. These counts do not identify you personally.
