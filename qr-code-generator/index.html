Tips for a QR code that scans first time

  • Keep strong contrast. Dark code on a light background. Light-on-dark codes fail on many phones; the tool warns you.
  • Print it at least 2 cm wide, bigger if people scan from a distance. A rough rule: width = scanning distance ÷ 10.
  • Test it with two different phones before you print hundreds.
  • Use High error correction if the code may get scratched or you'll put a small logo in the middle.

UPI QR codes

A UPI QR opens any UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) with your UPI ID filled in. Add an amount to fix the price, or leave it blank so the payer types it. Double-check your UPI ID: the payment goes wherever the code points.

Do these QR codes expire?

No. These are static QR codes: the link or details are stored inside the pattern itself, not on a server. They work forever. The flip side is you can't change where it points after printing; make a new one instead.

Frequently asked questions

Is my Wi-Fi password or UPI ID sent anywhere?

No. The QR code is drawn in your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded.

PNG or SVG?

PNG for WhatsApp, slides and documents. SVG for printing and designers: it stays sharp at any size.
