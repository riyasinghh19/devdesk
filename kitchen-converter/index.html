Why weight is more accurate

Flour can weigh anywhere from 110 g to 150 g a cup depending on how you fill it. Scooping straight from the bag packs it down. For baking, weigh if you can. If you must use cups, spoon the flour into the cup and level it with a knife.

Spoons

  • 1 tablespoon (tbsp) = 3 teaspoons (tsp) = 15 ml
  • 1 cup = 16 tablespoons
  • A teaspoon from your cutlery drawer is often only 3–4 ml, not 5. Use measuring spoons for baking powder and salt.

Frequently asked questions

Which cup does my recipe use?

American recipes use the US cup (about 237 ml). British, Australian and many Indian baking blogs use 250 ml. The difference is small for cooking but can matter in baking.

Are the gram values exact?

They're typical averages from standard references. Brands, humidity and how you fill the cup all change the real weight slightly.
