Why deposit before 5 April

PPF interest is worked out each month on the lowest balance between the 5th and the last day of the month, then added once a year on 31 March. A deposit made by 5 April earns interest for all 12 months. The same deposit made in March earns almost nothing that year. Over 15 years, that difference adds up to tens of thousands of rupees.

Key PPF rules

  • Lock-in: 15 full financial years from the end of the year you open the account.
  • Tax: exempt at all three stages (EEE). Deposits count for 80C in the old regime; interest and maturity are tax-free in both regimes.
  • Extensions: after maturity, extend in blocks of 5 years, with or without fresh deposits.
  • Partial withdrawal: allowed from the 7th financial year, up to 50% of an earlier balance.
  • Loan: available from the 3rd to the 6th year, up to 25% of the balance two years earlier.
  • Minimum: ₹500 a year to keep the account active.

Frequently asked questions

Will the rate stay at 7.1%?

No one knows. The government reviews it every quarter. It has stayed at 7.1% since April 2020, including the October–December 2026 quarter.

Can I open PPF for my child?

Yes, as guardian. But the ₹1.5 lakh yearly limit is shared between your own account and your minor child's.
