About

Why DevDesk exists

I'm Riya Singh, a Senior Data Engineer based in Pune with more than five years of building data pipelines on Azure, Snowflake and dbt. My work days are full of small, repetitive jobs: tidying a SQL query, decoding a cron schedule, converting a timestamp. Most online tools for these are slow, cluttered or want your data on their servers.

So I'm building my own, one tool a day for 30 days. Each one does a single job well, loads fast and runs entirely in your browser.

What you can expect

  • Privacy first. Text, files and numbers you enter are processed on your device and are not uploaded.
  • No sign-up. Open a tool and use it.
  • Plain explanations. Every tool comes with a short guide, so you learn the why as well as the how.
