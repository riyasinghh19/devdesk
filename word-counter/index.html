How the counts work

  • Words are groups of letters or numbers separated by spaces or punctuation. "Data-driven" counts as one word.
  • Characters count emoji and Indian-language letters as people see them, not as computer code units.
  • Reading time uses 230 words a minute; speaking time uses 140, a comfortable pace for a talk or video.

Frequently asked questions

Is my text saved or sent anywhere?

It stays in your browser. Your last text is kept on this device so it's still there if you refresh; Clear removes it.

Does it work for Hindi and other Indian languages?

Yes. Words and characters are counted correctly for Devanagari, Tamil, Bengali and other scripts.
