Why in-hand is less than CTC ÷ 12

CTC (cost to company) is everything your employer spends on you, including money you don't receive every month:

  • Employer PF (12% of basic) goes into your EPF account. It's your money, but you get it later.
  • Gratuity (4.81% of basic) is paid only if you leave after five or more years of service.
  • Variable pay or bonus is paid once or a few times a year, often depending on performance.
  • Insurance premiums and other benefits are paid to the insurer, not to you.

Then from your gross salary, employee PF (another 12% of basic), professional tax and income tax (TDS) are deducted.

A quick example

CTC ₹18 lakh, basic 50%, full PF, gratuity in CTC, new regime, Maharashtra: employer PF ₹1.08 lakh and gratuity ₹43,290 come out first. From the gross, employee PF ₹1.08 lakh, PT ₹2,500 and about ₹1.2 lakh of tax are deducted, leaving roughly ₹1.18 lakh a month.

Ways to raise your take-home

  • Choose the PF cap if your employer allows it (12% of ₹15,000 = ₹1,800 a month). You save less for retirement but take home more.
  • Employer NPS (up to 14% of basic) is tax-free in the new regime, which lowers TDS.
  • Compare regimes with the old vs new tax calculator.

Frequently asked questions

Is the in-hand the same every month?

Usually close. Employers spread TDS across the year, and it can rise in the last quarter if you submit investment proofs late or get a bonus.

Does HRA change my in-hand?

HRA is part of your gross salary. In the old regime, its exempt part lowers your tax. In the new regime, HRA is fully taxed.
