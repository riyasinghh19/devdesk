Image compressor

Form says "photo under 50 KB"? Drop your image, pick the size and download. Everything happens on your device, so your photos are never uploaded.

Common size limits on Indian forms

FormPhotoSignature
Many government exams (SSC, banking, railways)20–50 KB10–20 KB
Passport SevaUp to 1 MB, JPG—
Job portals and HR systemsUsually up to 1–2 MB—

Limits change between notifications, so always check the form's instructions for size and pixel dimensions.

How it works

  1. Your image is drawn onto a canvas in your browser, resized to the maximum width you chose.
  2. For a size target, the tool tries different JPG or WebP quality levels and keeps the best one under the limit.
  3. If even low quality is too big, it shrinks the dimensions step by step until the file fits.

Tips

  • JPG is best for photos. PNG is for screenshots and logos with flat colours; it can't hit small size targets.
  • For a signature, crop tightly first, so the pixels are spent on the ink and not the white paper.
  • Photo metadata (EXIF), including phone location, is removed in the compressed copy.

Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Compression runs in your browser. You can switch off the internet after the page loads and it still works.

Why does my photo look sideways?

Modern browsers apply the phone's rotation automatically. If an older browser shows it sideways, rotate it in your gallery first.
