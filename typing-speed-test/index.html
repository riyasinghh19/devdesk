Typing speed test

Start typing and the clock starts. Many data-entry and typist job tests ask for 30 to 40 words per minute at 95% accuracy or better.

The timer starts with your first key.

Your result

How the score is worked out

  • Words per minute (WPM) counts every 5 correctly typed characters, including spaces, as one word. This is the standard used by typing tests worldwide.
  • Raw WPM counts everything you typed, right or wrong.
  • Accuracy is the share of characters you typed correctly.

What is a good typing speed?

WPMLevel
Under 25Beginner. Practise touch typing without looking at the keys.
25–40Average. Enough for most office work.
40–60Good. Comfortably passes most job typing tests.
60–80Fast. Typical of people who type all day.
80+Very fast.

Tips to type faster

  • Keep your fingers on the home row (ASDF and JKL;) and return to it after every key.
  • Accuracy first. Speed follows once you stop correcting mistakes.
  • Practise 10 minutes a day rather than an hour once a week.
  • Look at the screen, not the keyboard.

Frequently asked questions

Do government typing tests use the same method?

Most use 5 characters per word too, but some count only full correct words or deduct for each mistake. Check the rules for your exam.

Does Backspace count against me?

No. Only what is in the box when time runs out counts, so fixing a mistake helps your accuracy.
