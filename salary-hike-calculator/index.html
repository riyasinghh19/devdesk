How to calculate a salary hike

New CTC = Current CTC × (1 + hike% ÷ 100)
Hike %  = (New CTC − Current CTC) ÷ Current CTC × 100

Example: ₹12 LPA with a 10% hike becomes ₹13.2 LPA. An offer of ₹16 LPA on a current ₹12 LPA is a 33.3% hike.

Compare like with like

  • Fixed vs variable: a ₹16 LPA offer with ₹3 L variable is ₹13 L fixed. Compare fixed pay to fixed pay.
  • Joining and retention bonuses are one-time. They don't carry into next year's hike.
  • Benefits: insurance, NPS and gratuity are often counted in CTC. Check your in-hand with the CTC to in-hand calculator.
  • ESOPs or RSUs are worth something only if they vest and can be sold. Value them conservatively.

What counts as a good hike in India?

Annual appraisals at most companies have averaged around 8–10% in recent years, with top performers getting more. When switching jobs, 20–40% is a common range, and more for in-demand skills. These are broad averages, not promises; your industry and level matter a lot.

Frequently asked questions

What is "real" hike?

It's your raise after inflation eats into it. A 6% hike with 5% inflation increases what you can buy by only about 1%.

How do I ask for a bigger hike?

Bring evidence: outcomes you delivered with numbers, the market rate for your role, and the scope you've taken on. The resume bullet builder helps you phrase your achievements.
