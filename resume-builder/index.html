Why a simple layout wins

Fancy two-column templates with icons and skill bars look good to people but often scramble when applicant tracking systems read them. Dates end up next to the wrong job and skills disappear. That's why seven of the twelve templates here use one column, standard headings and real text, so both the software and the recruiter read them the way you intended. The five Designer templates are for when a person, not a portal, will read your PDF.

Tips while you write

  • Lead with your strongest recent role. Recruiters spend seconds on the first scan.
  • Three to six bullets per job. Start each with a verb and include a number where you can.
  • Match the job's words. Run your finished resume through the ATS Score Checker against the job you want.
  • One page per 8 to 10 years of experience is a good rule of thumb.

Frequently asked questions

Which template should I pick?

The first seven are ATS-safe. Classic and Compact suit almost any role. Modern, Banner and Timeline add colour for tech, startup and creative-leaning jobs. Executive and Minimal suit senior, finance and consulting roles. Those seven are ATS-safe: one column, one clear reading order. The five Designer templates (Studio, Profile, Evergreen, Split and Midnight) add a sidebar and an optional photo, which looks great when you email your resume to a person, but two columns can confuse some job-portal ATS software. A good habit: send a Designer PDF by email and upload an ATS-safe one to portals. You can switch templates and colours any time without retyping.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every template, colour and download is free, with no watermark and no sign-up. Download as many versions as you like.

Where is my resume saved?

Only in this browser. Your draft and photo never leave your device, and the PDF is made locally too. Clearing your browser data, or clicking Start blank, removes the draft.

The print window opened. What now?

Set the destination to Save as PDF, set margins to None or Default, tick Background graphics so colours and sidebars appear, then click Save. On a phone, choose Print, then Save as PDF or Share.
