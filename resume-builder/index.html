Why a simple layout wins

Fancy two-column templates with icons and skill bars look good to people but often scramble when applicant tracking systems read them. Dates end up next to the wrong job and skills disappear. This builder uses one column, standard headings and real text, so both the software and the recruiter read it the way you intended.

Tips while you write

  • Lead with your strongest recent role. Recruiters spend seconds on the first scan.
  • Three to six bullets per job. Start each with a verb and include a number where you can.
  • Match the job's words. Run your finished resume through the ATS Score Checker against the job you want.
  • One page per 8 to 10 years of experience is a good rule of thumb.

Frequently asked questions

Which template should I pick?

Classic suits most roles. Modern adds a touch of colour for tech and startup jobs. Compact fits more on one page if you have lots of experience. Elegant uses a serif font that suits finance, law and senior roles. All four use one column and real text, so ATS software reads every one of them correctly.
