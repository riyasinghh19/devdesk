Example: ₹1,180 including 18% GST. GST = 1180 × 18 ÷ 118 = ₹180, so the price before GST is ₹1,000. A common mistake is to take 18% of ₹1,180 (₹212.40), which is wrong.

GST rates in India

Since 22 September 2025, most goods and services fall into two main rates, with a higher rate for a few items:

RateTypical items
0%Fresh food, milk, health and life insurance for individuals
5%Packaged food, everyday essentials, many medicines
18%Most goods and services, including IT and professional services
40%Luxury and "sin" goods such as tobacco and aerated drinks
3% / 0.25%Gold and jewellery / rough diamonds

The older 12% and 28% rates were merged into these. Always check the rate for your exact item (its HSN or SAC code) on the GST portal.

CGST, SGST and IGST

When the seller and buyer are in the same state, GST is split equally: half is CGST (central) and half is SGST (state). At 18%, that's 9% + 9%. When they're in different states, the full 18% is charged as IGST. The total is the same either way.

Frequently asked questions

Do freelancers have to charge GST?

You must register once your turnover from services crosses ₹20 lakh a year (₹10 lakh in some special-category states). Exporting services to clients abroad is usually zero-rated, but registration rules still apply. Check with a chartered accountant.

How do I round GST on an invoice?

Calculate tax per line item and round to two decimals. The calculator shows paise so you can match your invoice.
