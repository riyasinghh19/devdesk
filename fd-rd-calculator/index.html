How FD interest is calculated

Maturity = P × (1 + r / n)^(n × t)

P is the deposit, r the yearly rate as a decimal, n how many times a year interest compounds (4 for quarterly) and t the time in years. ₹5 lakh at 7% for 3 years, compounded quarterly, grows to about ₹6.16 lakh.

How RD interest is calculated

Each monthly instalment earns compound interest for the months it stays in the account. The first instalment earns for the full tenure, the last for just one month. Banks compound RD interest quarterly, and the calculator adds up every instalment the same way.

Things to know

  • Senior citizens usually get 0.25–0.75% extra. Enter the higher rate.
  • TDS: banks deduct 10% TDS when interest crosses ₹50,000 a year (₹1 lakh for senior citizens). Submit Form 15G/15H if your total income is below the taxable limit.
  • Tax-saver FDs have a 5-year lock-in and qualify for 80C in the old regime only.
  • Deposit insurance: DICGC insures up to ₹5 lakh per depositor per bank, including interest.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my bank's figure slightly different?

Banks count tenure in exact days and some use different rounding. For tenures under 6 months, banks usually pay simple interest. The difference is normally a few rupees.

FD or debt mutual fund?

Both are taxed at your slab rate now. FDs give a fixed, known return. Debt funds can give slightly more but the return isn't guaranteed.
