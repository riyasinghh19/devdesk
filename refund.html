Refunds & cancellations

Last updated 27 October 2026

Pro tools deliver a digital result (an ATS report or a resume PDF) straight after payment, so orders can't be cancelled once the result is delivered.

When you get a full refund

  • You were charged but didn't receive your report or PDF because of a problem on our side.
  • You were charged more than once for the same order.

How to ask

Email riyasingh10x@gmail.com within 7 days of payment with your Razorpay payment ID and a short description. We reply within 2 business days.

How you're refunded

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method (UPI, card, net banking or wallet) through Razorpay, usually within 5 to 7 business days.
