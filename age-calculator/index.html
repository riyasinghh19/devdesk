How age is counted

The calculator counts full years first, then full months, then the days left over, the same way government forms and HR systems do.

Age "as on" a date

Many applications ask for your age on a fixed date rather than today. For example, a recruitment notice might say "age as on 1 August 2026: 21 to 32 years". Set "Age as on" to that date and check the result.

Born on 29 February?

In non-leap years, the calculator treats your birthday as 28 February for the countdown. Legally, many places count it as 1 March; check the rules for your form.

Frequently asked questions

Is my date of birth stored?

Only in your own browser, so it's filled in next time. Nothing is uploaded.

Why does my age in days differ from another site?

Some tools count the end date as an extra day. This calculator counts the days between the two dates, not including the end date.
