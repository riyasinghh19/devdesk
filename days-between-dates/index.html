Should I include the end date?

By default the count is the difference between the dates: from Monday to Friday is 4 days. If you're counting days on which something happens, like a leave from Monday to Friday, tick "include the end date" to get 5.

Working days

Working days skip weekends and any holidays you list. Indian banks and many government offices close on the 2nd and 4th Saturday, so there's an option for that. Add your company's holiday list to get an exact count.

Common uses

  • Days left until a project deadline or exam.
  • Working days in a sprint or billing period.
  • 90 days from today for a notice period, visa or warranty (use "Add or subtract days").
  • Days between two transactions for interest calculations.

Frequently asked questions

Are leap years handled?

Yes. The count uses real calendar dates, so 29 February is counted in leap years.

Does it know Indian public holidays?

Holidays differ by state and employer, so add your own list. Paste dates one per line.
